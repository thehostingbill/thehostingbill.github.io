THE HOSTING BILL

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

What this covers

The Hosting Bill Uploader is a private tool used only by the operator of The Hosting Bill to upload and manage videos on the operator's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

The tool signs in only to the operator's own Google account. With that permission it uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and changes their visibility. It does not access any other person's Google or YouTube data.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored on the operator's own server with access restricted to the operator. Nothing obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Viewers

This site and the benchmark repository do not use cookies or tracking. Watching videos on YouTube is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/thehostingbill/benchmarks/issues.
